# Attribution Correction: Version 0.1.1

Published as an attribution-only correction of version 0.1.0.

The original public byline named Aravind Karthigeyan. He has clarified that
he directed and published this AI-generated project but did not write its
proof or manuscript. The current artifact removes that human author byline
and prominently credits the generating systems:

- Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`).
- Codex using GPT-6 Astra with Ultra reasoning effort (`gpt-6-astra`, `ultra`).
- Separately imported Astra Pro contributions, whose backend model is not
  independently verified.

These names describe AI generation, not human scholarly authorship, legal
personhood, provider endorsement, or an external review. The role of AI went
beyond editorial assistance and included mathematical work, certificate
generation, formalization, checks, and exposition. No per-file attribution is
invented where the records do not establish it.

The mathematical proof, exact certificates, verification inputs, and original
verification results are unchanged. This version is not a new full Lean build.
Its new paper, documentation, and manifest have their own bytes and checksums.

The v0.1.0 Git tag and archive remain historical records; they are not silently
rewritten. The Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23192843` identifies those older
v0.1.0 files, not the corrected v0.1.1 GitHub release. The depositor requested
retaining his name on that Zenodo record, with explicit AI-generation and
role disclosure in its description. A Zenodo creator listing must not be
read as a claim that he personally wrote the paper or formal proof.

Zenodo forbids AI tools in its creator/contributor fields, and arXiv does not
permit AI tools as authors. The generation credits in this software artifact
are not a workaround for those policies. No arXiv submission is represented
as ready or made. Zenodo additionally requires a human-conducted research
basis and human accountability; retaining a human creator name and disclosing
AI use do not by themselves establish eligibility. This correction does not
certify compliance with those requirements; host clarification may be needed.
The existing license notices are retained for continuity;
copyright ownership and manuscript authorship are distinct questions.
