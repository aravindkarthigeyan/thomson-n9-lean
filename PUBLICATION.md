## Historical Evidence

Redacted records remain historical records. Hashes within them refer to the
original, pre-redaction artifacts unless explicitly labeled otherwise. Some
old integrity scripts expect those originals and are not applicable to the
redacted publication. Use the new public inventory verifier for this release,
and rerun the mathematical checkers for independent verification.

Private home-directory components are replaced by `REDACTED`. Verbatim private
continuation messages in supervision receipts and their historical scripts
are replaced by a redaction marker. These substitutions do not create a new
kernel result and do not modify mathematical proof sources. Historical scripts
with substituted setup paths are provenance, not runnable fresh-machine setup.

The source archive and unredacted receipts remain preserved locally. They are
not attached publicly by default. A public ZIP has its own name and hash.
